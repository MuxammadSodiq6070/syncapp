Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026
Zero-Knowledge Principle: Sync is built so that neither the gateway servers nor any third party can read your communications or access your identity.

1. Information We Collect

Sync is architected to minimize data collection by design:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not require or collect your phone number, email address, contact list, or real name.
  • Cryptographic Identity: Your account identifier is an Ed25519 public key generated exclusively on your local device.
  • Profile Data: An optional public username and display name chosen by you.

2. End-to-End Encryption

All direct chats, group messages, and media files are end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) on your device before transmission using industry-standard cryptography (XChaCha20-Poly1305, X25519, BLAKE2b via Monocypher). The relay servers only see opaque ciphertext and routing metadata necessary for message delivery.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

  • Camera: Used strictly when you take photos to send in chats.
  • Microphone: Used strictly when you record voice notes.
  • Notifications: Used strictly to alert you of incoming messages.

We do not access or record sensor data in the background.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

Sync contains zero third-party trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry. Your messages and metadata are never sold, rented, or shared with commercial entities.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can permanently erase your cryptographic keys, all local chats, messages, and downloaded media at any time by navigating to:

Settings > Delete account

Deleting your account permanently wipes your local cryptographic private key and prevents any future decryption of past messages.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via your project support channel or repository.
